Scope and limitations

  • Feature SHAP explains the raw logit given fixed account memories, not all historical or static node features.
  • Exact values apply to the sampled training background. Correlated-feature hybrids may be unrealistic; these are model explanations, not causal findings.
  • Legacy encoders were reconstructed. The original dataset and software versions are required for original-run reproducibility; hashes record this run, not proof of training-data identity.
  • Historical effects cover only selected earlier direct-endpoint events in a bounded window; they are not additive and are not a complete graph explanation.
  • The original model's batch boundaries are preserved. Its timestamp ties can cross batch boundaries; this adapter does not change that behavior.
  • Explanations and transaction identifiers stay in local files. This does not provide a formal privacy guarantee.
  • A threshold, when available, is read from the checkpoint directory's metrics.json; keep matching artifacts together.
